# Changelog

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-04
### Added
- Three new stages, each with its own floor, decor, waves, events, bosses and final boss:
  **Sunken Crypt** (darker, the dead hit harder), **Ember Depths** (lava, demons, the Ashen Wyrm) and
  **Hollow Keep** (the King's halls, hardest). Each unlocks by surviving 10 minutes on the previous one,
  and pays more gold (x1.25, x1.5, x1.8).
- Stage picker on the hero screen, best time and wins per stage, stages in the Collection.
- Runtime music composer: the new stages get a freshly written tune (and boss theme) every run, in the stage's mood.
- New boss behaviour: the dragon's fire breath. The Fiend joins the boss roster.
- Stage props from Enter the Fluffy's sprite sheet (pillars, columns, urns, obelisks, crystals, fire drums...).

### Changed
- HUD: the timer stays centred and weapon icons are smaller, so six weapons no longer crowd it.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-04
### Added
- First playable version: 15-minute runs, 5 heroes, 9 weapons with 9 evolutions, 10 passives, chests, 5 bosses,
  swarm and ring events, braziers, power-up shop, collection with discovered recipes, hard mode, original chiptune music.
